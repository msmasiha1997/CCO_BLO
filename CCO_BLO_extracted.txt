Introduction
Optimization in an Uncertain World
How can a robot minimize its energy
consumption while navigating, given that its
sensor readings are uncertain and it must still
avoid obstacles with high probability?
How do we manage a power grid when
wind/solar energy is unpredictable?
Chance Constrained Optimization (CCO) can
be seen as a framework for handling
uncertainty in optimization problems.

Introduction
CCO Real-World Applications
Energy Management:Ensuring power supply meets demand with high
probability, given the uncertainty of renewable sources like wind and solar.
Finance:Designing investment portfolios where the probability of losing more
than a certain amount is kept low.
Robotics & Autonomous Systems:Path planning where a robot must avoid
obstacles with high confidence despite noisy sensors.
Supply Chain Management:Managing inventory to meet uncertain customer
demand without frequent stockouts.
